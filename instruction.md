# Pekoe Trail Walk: instructions

Browser 3D walk of **Pekoe Trail Stage 1** (Ceylon Tea Museum, Hanthana → Galaha Post Office), built from our own walk. Version 1 covers Stage 1 only.

## Sources

- Trail site: https://thepekoetrail.org/
- Stage 1 page: https://thepekoetrail.org/pekoe-stages/stage-01/
- Stage 1 map: https://thepekoetrail.org/wp-content/uploads/Stage_01-768x802.png

Five people, modelled from `photos/team.jpeg`. Walking order, front to back:

## Our story along the trail

| Where | What happened |
|---|---|
| First km, left side | Saw Hanthana Sandagiri Maha Seya |
| Along the trail | Met a few local people |
| 7–8 km | Very light rain. Dinusha used an umbrella, Sudesh a raincoat, the others had nothing |
| ~8 km, right side | Small Hindu kovil. We filled our water bottles and gave 5 chocolates to the kids at the temple |
| Just after the kovil | Gave 2 more chocolates to other kids, and they gave us a flower bouquet |

## Start: Ceylon Tea Museum

- Nameboard "Ceylon Tea Museum" on the building and at the trailhead.
- Stage 1 map as a banner beside the path at the start.
- Trail logo at the start and the end. Also used as the favicon.
- Tourists arriving at the museum.
- Top-floor restaurant and tea shop with tourists.
- A viewing telescope for looking at the surroundings.

## Along the trail

- Foreign tourists met on the path several times.
- Women plucking tea in the tea fields.
- Guide marks for navigation:
  - painted arrows on trees and rocks;
  - signposts at key junctions.
- More houses in the last 2 km into Galaha.

## Controls

- **Buttons:**
  - start again from the beginning
  - walk back
  - walk / stop
  - speed 1× / 2×
  - run
  - look left / right
- **Landmarks:** click a marker to see its information.
- **Keyboard:**

  | Key | Action |
  |---|---|
  | W / ↑ | Walk |
  | S / ↓ | Back |
  | Space | Walk / stop |
  | Shift | Run |
  | A / D | Look left / right |
  | E | Reopen a landmark |

## Status

All items above are done in `index.html`.

To change a landmark's position, side or text, edit `tools/build-landmarks.py` and re-run it (see `requirement.md`).

Missing photos and files are listed in `requirement.md`.
